## From the stone age to the stars

**Createch 2** is a long-form survival and automation campaign. You don't spawn next to a machine that solves everything. You learn the land, master metallurgy, build factories, and connect them with railways. Then you take the whole industrial system into space.

There are **17 progression ages**, and they are connected by a simple principle: **everything you build should matter later**. Your first settlement becomes the foundation of a civilization stretching across worlds.

### 01 — Survive the land

![A TerraFirmaCraft-style landscape and stone ruins in Createch 2](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/1790/181/createch-2-png.png)

Start with **TerraFirmaCraft**: food, seasons, prospecting, pottery, charcoal, and metallurgy. Survive first. Industry comes after you've earned it.

### 02 — Engineer your progression

![The Createch 2 quest book showing the industrial progression tiers and research path](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/1878/819/over-1200-quests-png.png)

Advance through research, quests, and deeply connected manufacturing. **Create and Greate** turn shafts, belts, presses, foundries, power systems, and component production into a factory you can actually understand and expand.

### 03 — Build the infrastructure to leave Earth

![Createch 2 explorer and machinery on the surface of Mars](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/1790/202/mars-png.png)

**Rail before flight.** Mines, oil fields, specialized plants, and train networks must support your growing industry. Then come advanced electronics, chemistry, rockets, and off-world resource extraction.

### 04 — Make the galaxy part of your factory

![The rocky Venus landscape in Createch 2](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/1908/978/untitled-design-12-png.png)

The Moon, Mars, Venus, and worlds beyond become places to explore, mine, and industrialize. Space logistics, radiation, new environments, and eventually **Stargates** transform the scale of what you can build. Research and magic develop alongside technology, leading toward **Re:Avaritia and Infinity**.

> **Survive the land. Build the factory. Connect the world. Cross the galaxy. Engineer Infinity.**

## Get started

1. **[Install Createch 2 through CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/createch-2)** — use the official profile rather than downloading this source repository.
2. **Use Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge** as supplied with the pack. Allow extra time for the first launch of this large expert modpack.
3. **Plan for approximately 10 GB of allocated RAM** if your computer has sufficient memory available; actual requirements vary with settings and play style.
4. **Follow the in-game quest book** to begin the survival and technology progression.

The pack is built around a long, deliberate campaign—not instant creative-style automation or early unrestricted flight.

## For developers & contributors

This GitHub repository contains the **pack's configuration and development sources**. It is **not** a standalone, installable CurseForge export and does not contain the mod JARs or a ready-to-run instance.

<details>
<summary><strong>View repository layout and development notes</strong></summary>

```text
config/             Mod configuration, quests, menus, and resources
defaultconfigs/     Default world and server settings
kubejs/             Runtime startup, server, and client scripts / data / assets
tacz/               Gun-pack resources and customized animations
scripts/            Development generators, libraries, and registries
mod-inventory.json  Installed mod inventory and provenance metadata
```

The tools under [`scripts/`](scripts/README.md) were developed around an external local environment, with instance bindings, supporting evidence, and installed mod files that are intentionally not included here. This repository should not be treated as an independently validated pack build or installer.

For normal gameplay, **install using [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/modpacks/createch-2)**.
