# Standardized Reconstruction

Standardized Reconstruction is optional Age 10 recovery infrastructure. First build and prove the relevant industries normally. Then complete **PRE-03B — Standardized Reconstruction**, craft the MultiBuilder, charge it from the Electro Energetics-authorized FE handoff, and supply every real block.

The approved 1.1.28 library contains exactly three Createch standards:

- **Precision Beneficiation Plant** places the proven MI/Mekanism machine row and foundations.
- **Protected Process Substation** places a transformer, switching, protection, meters, grounding, and connector hardware.
- **Rail Freight Terminal** places proven Railcraft loaders, a rolling machine, a Create station, storage, and a control computer.

The tool stores 10 MFE, transfers at 25 kFE/t, and spends 2,500 FE for every placed block. It consumes exact materials. Mekanism pipe tiers are not interchangeable. AE2 may source or autocraft materials only when the player already has legal patterns and inputs.

All bundled example structures are filtered out. Wiring, pipes, rails, schedules, controls, pollution management, chunk placement, defenses, site design, and commissioning remain player work. A template cannot complete a first-time progression proof.

For the recovery proof, dismantle or damage one approved module, isolate it, stage spares, reconstruct it, reconnect utilities, and demonstrate real production after restart. Ownership-bound dismantling returns only structures placed by that player; test rotation and chunk boundaries before using a standard on a shared site.
