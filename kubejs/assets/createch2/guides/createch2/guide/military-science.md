# Military Science

Military Science is an optional Research’d page. It connects primitive defense to industrial ordnance, instrumented ballistics, automated defense, strategic deterrence, and exotic threat response without becoming part of ordinary Infinity.

The governing rule is simple: **civilian industry creates military capability**.

- Metallurgy and mechanics support durable arms.
- Heavy steel and process chemistry support artillery.
- Computation and metrology support firing solutions and guidance.
- Aviation and high-energy grids support automated defense.
- Nuclear mastery supports strategic deterrence.
- Frontier, fusion, and esoteric science support the final exotic branch.

Ordnance Reports use the native Research’d Research Pack item. Their production grows from handwritten combat observations into MI test cells and proving grounds. Test instruments marked reusable are present with zero consumption; targets, ammunition, cores, charges, and paper are consumed.

The strategic proof is possession of a weapon, never detonation. Defensive investment is valuable for remote mines, launch sites, reactors, research campuses, and gateways, but it remains a player choice.
