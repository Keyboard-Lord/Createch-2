# The Chemical Plant

The refinery and chemical district are permanent infrastructure, not disposable age rooms.

The core feedstock tree begins with crude distillation. Naphtha cracking supports olefins; propylene supports synthetic rubber; ethylene supports polymer and insulation lines. Hydrogen, oxygen, chlorine, hydrochloric acid, sulfuric acid, lubricant, rubber, polyethylene, and PVC should have labeled storage and more than one downstream consumer.

Later campaigns reuse the same language:

- titanium upgrades chlorine and hydrochloric-acid handling;
- PGM separation adds acid leaching, purification, and reagent recovery;
- nuclear work adds isotope, coolant, waste, and reprocessing chemistry;
- Quantum adds cryofluid, fusion isotopes, plasma, and UU Matter.

Design for isolation, overflow, reagent return, and a cold restart. A large stored stack is not proof of a well-behaved plant.
