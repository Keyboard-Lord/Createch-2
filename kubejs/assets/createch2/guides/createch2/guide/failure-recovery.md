# Failure and Recovery

Before expanding a plant, prove that it can stop safely and restart without quest rewards or command replacement.

1. Isolate power, pressure, heat, fuel, and hazardous inventory independently.
2. Preserve a manual route around automated transport and control.
3. Stage replacement seals, conductors, controls, filters, and machine-specific wear parts outside the live line.
4. Clear blocked outputs before reconnecting inputs.
5. Restart upstream utilities first, then processing, then automated demand.
6. Replenish the recovery stock after the test.

For Electro Energetics, test protection and black start. For chemistry, verify reagent reserves and waste routing. For rail, retain a manual transfer path. For nuclear systems, keep shutdown, cooling, radiation measurement, waste, and restart as separate responsibilities.
