# Research Production

Research’d records what the civilization understands. FTB Quests records what the factory has physically proved. This guide explains how evidence moves between them.

The Copper-era Research Lab is intentionally simple. Research Pack production is what grows into an industry:

> handwritten observation → workshop experiment → instrumented test cell → automated laboratory → specialist facility → frontier research industry

Primitive recipes remain legal forever. They are low-yield fallbacks, not the permanent best method. Later research unlocks machine routes that produce the same evidence in larger batches, so an old science district can be expanded or rebuilt without invalidating its output.

## Evidence families

- **Kinetic Data** measures motion, torque, mechanisms, motors, and mechatronics.
- **Material Specimens** qualify metals, structures, high-temperature materials, and exotic matter.
- **Process Samples** record controlled thermal, refinery, polymer, chemical, and cryogenic work.
- **Grid Telemetry** comes from measured generation, conductors, loads, protection, and high-power behavior.
- **Computational Models** are produced by analog, networked, and quantum computation.
- **Logistics Reports** record manifests, rail service, freight throughput, digital demand, and extraplanetary transport.
- **Propulsion Telemetry** comes from motor, rocket-engine, thruster, and booster test cycles.
- **Geological Surveys** combine prospecting, crushing, deep extraction, and planetary resource models.
- **Nuclear Assays** measure radiation, isotopes, reactor fuel, reprocessing, and fusion fuels.
- **Frontier Observations** study Starcores, accelerator experiments, Strange Matter, and extreme physics.
- **Esoteric Records** preserve repeatable evidence from botanical, ritual, occult, and frontier supernatural work.

## Test articles and consumption

The recipe should match the experiment. Material coupons, fuel samples, chemicals, and ore samples may be destroyed. Expensive hardware used as an instrument is usually a reusable catalyst: motors, computers, gauges, locomotives, rocket engines, dosimeters, Starcores, and accelerator controllers survive their test cycle.

In recipe viewers, a zero-consumption machine input is the retained test article. The operation still consumes time, power, fuel, coolant, reagents, paper, or physical samples.

## Laboratory productivity

The first lab operates at baseline productivity. Electromechanical Conversion, Networked Control, Reactor Instrumentation, and Megaproject Governance improve Research’d Lab Productivity. These bonuses represent better measurement, procedures, computation, and institutional review; the research factory remains the primary throughput bottleneck.

Build research departments like any other industrial district: provide power and fluid isolation, input and output buffers, visible test articles, overflow handling, and a manual recovery path.
