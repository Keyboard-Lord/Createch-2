# Environment and Resources

TerraFirmaCraft owns Terra geology, grades, first acquisition, and early metallurgy. Resource simulations, bees, drills, ProjectE, and planetary deposits are later institutions; none may erase first discovery or precision processing.

Environmental engineering grows with the factory:

- observe pollution and identify the causal process;
- contain emissions and wastes at the source;
- install filtration, scrubbing, protected storage, and monitoring;
- recover reagents or useful side streams where the process supports it;
- restore soil, biomass, or water after the industrial event;
- retain a safe shutdown route when mitigation is offline.

Fire and weather have one chain of authority. TFC owns ignition conditions, rain, seasons, and biological climate; Burnt owns spread and aftermath after an active source lights the surroundings. TFC firepits, kilns, forges, and heavy furnaces join that consequence layer only while burning. AdPother measures their combustion and AdChimneys routes the emissions; a chimney moves pollution, it does not erase it. Snow Real Magic only shapes TFC's seasonal snow. Cold Sweat with TFC Cold Sweat owns player temperature, including authored planetary extremes.

Renewable routes become visible after first acquisition. Tree farms, biomass, plant oil, solar, wind, water, and Productive Bees reduce steady operating cost, but their recipes preserve the relevant research and material authority. ProjectE is a late matter-management tool, not a substitute for precision chemistry or first proof.
