# Rail Freight Operations

Create trains are the preferred railway platform. Railcraft supplies mature rail industry: coke and treatment, rolling, loaders, unloaders, tanks, and freight-terminal hardware. Its rolling stock can remain useful, but it is not a permanent tax on using Create trains.

A mature terminal separates five responsibilities:

1. a Create station, track, signal, schedule, and train assembly;
2. Railcraft production and transfer hardware;
3. storage buffers sized for at least one interrupted service cycle;
4. Electro Energetics distribution and protection for electric terminal loads;
5. local controls plus a manual transfer path.

Rail is high-throughput persistent freight. Aviation is terrain-independent mobility. Rockets are first access and heavy geological expedition work. Stargates become mature logistics only after discovery. None replaces the others.

For recovery, stop schedules, isolate loaders, clear the blocked buffer, run one manual transfer, then restore the timetable and prove a complete loaded-and-unloaded trip.
