# Createch 2 Guide

This manual explains **how Createch 2 expects its systems to work together**. It complements, but does not replace, the other progression surfaces:

- **FTB Quests** says what to build and which physical proof completes an age.
- **Research’d** records what the civilization knows and owns the configured recipe unlocks. Its Research Packs begin as observations and scale into machine experiments; FTB Quests still owns physical age proof.
- **This guide** explains system authority, plant layout, failure recovery, and why pack recipes differ from native mod recipes.

When a native mod guide and Createch 2 disagree about acquisition or progression, the live Createch recipe and questbook are authoritative.

FTB Quests fluid tasks consume the listed amount when submitted through the quest screen. They verify delivery, not that a tank still holds the amount afterward; build and label the buffer described by the quest before submitting.
