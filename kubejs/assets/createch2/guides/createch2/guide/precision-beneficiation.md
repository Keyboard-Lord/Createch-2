# Precision Beneficiation

At Digital Age, **CHE-05B — Precision Beneficiation** opens one bounded pilot: tetrahedrite. This is not a universal Mekanism 5× switch.

1. Macerate graded TFC tetrahedrite in MI. Poor, normal, and rich feed recover more powder than the quern; sulfur dust is the useful side stream.
2. Dissolve ten tetrahedrite powder with Mekanism sulfuric acid into dirty copper slurry.
3. Wash the slurry with the Chemical Washer.
4. Crystallize the clean stream.
5. Use the native Mekanism crystal → shard → clump → dirty dust → dust sequence, then return the copper to the normal metallurgy economy.

The TFC quern and ordinary metallurgy remain available as the low-infrastructure fallback. Native Mekanism ore/raw shortcuts into its high-multiplication copper chain are closed; the internal chemical stages remain intact.

Recovered `tfmg:sulfur_dust` feeds the installed sulfuric-acid chemistry. If the acid plant stops, beneficiation should notice.

Run this district from a protected Electro Energetics process bus. The Device Connector is the FE handoff; Mekanism or generic FE cables are not an alternate distribution authority.
