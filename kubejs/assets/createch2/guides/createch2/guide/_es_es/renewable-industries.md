# Renewable Industries

Renewables are optional efficiency industries after legitimate first acquisition. They reward permanent operations without turning early scarcity into a one-block bypass.

- Managed forestry and crop systems supply biomass, charcoal, oils, rubber, and biopolymers.
- Wind, water, solar, and recovered process heat reduce fuel demand where their real infrastructure is appropriate.
- Productive Bees amplify already-discovered resources through explicit breeding and processing routes.
- Planetary apiaries require the destination, parent species, and processed samples defined by the planetary authority.

## Controlled agriculture

EI farmers mechanize real TFC fields: native soil variants, crop block entities, climate, hydration, nutrients, yield,
expiry, and calendar maturity remain authoritative. Irrigation consumes semantic fresh water; fertilizer adds native
N/P/K without directly aging crops; failed planting keeps its seed; pickable crops regrow; and pumpkin or melon vines
remain while their separate fruit is collected. Powered composting consumes a complete TFC green/brown balance for one
day at 4 EU/t and produces real TFC compost. It is an engineered acceleration, not instant vanilla bonemeal.

Design for intermittency: buffer energy and feedstock, shed noncritical demand, and retain black-start generation. A renewable route is healthy only when its acquisition, processing, byproducts, and recovery plan remain visible.

## Five apiary milestones

1. **Field handling:** use cages and honey treats after ordinary field acquisition. Keep reference stock and flowers; bees do not replace TFC prospecting.
2. **Mechanical processing:** the manual Create centrifuge and later sturdy steel cages turn hand collection into repeatable comb handling. This is the first processing floor, before a powered apiary.
3. **Controlled breeding:** LV incubation supplies managed reproduction. Space-age breeding equipment uses oxygen infrastructure and powered handling rather than granting planetary resources on its own.
4. **Digital apiary:** the Age 10 Industrial Apiary Shell and Comb Processing Floor combine the advanced hive, powered centrifuge, modular handling and optional ME exports. AE2 requests and buffers production; stock, flowers and the proper bees remain physical inputs.
5. **Specialist stock and records:** planetary heated processing requires real Venus/Mercury material. Visit the Bumblezone for royal jelly before assembling the gene indexer/extractor and organizing the Age 13 Apiary Records Office. Genetic records and destination-processed samples support reliable supply after discovery.

Bees remain optional production relief. Keep a non-bee first-acquisition and recovery route. A planetary recipe name alone is not evidence that it requires a visit; its real inputs and other gates determine availability.
